Circle of Fifths Interactive

Illustrations of notes in the 12-tone and microtonal worlds.

This app provides an opportunity to explore 12-tone and 31-tone equal temperaments.

This standalone HTML file is best suited for desktop fullscreen usage.

Keyboard Shortcuts

Click credit
Show this About screen

Click the illustration credit at the bottom of the main view.

Esc or ','
Go back

Exit or Show this About screen.

Click note
Select a note

Click a note to select it. Click it again to deselect it. You can select multiple notes.

Click + Drag
Move selected note

Drag a selected note to another position. Drop on an unselected note to replace it, or on an already selected note to merge. Release in blank space to remove the dragged note.

← / →
Move selection anticlockwise or clockwise

Move the selected figure by one position in the current circle's order.

Space
Play selected notes or Stop playing

Play the selected notes as a chord or stop playback. You can also use the button in the centre of the circle. In 31 EDO, Space confirms a pending pitch ID instead. While About is open, Space only stops playback that is already active.

Right-click note
Switch note spelling

In 12 EDO, right-click spelling changes are available only in manual mode. In 31 EDO, they remain available as before. Shift-right-click restores the default spelling.

Shift + Click title
Switch to microtonality or back

Switch between the 12-EDO and 31-EDO worlds.

Click title
Switch circle, or show a Circle List in microtonality

In 12 EDO, switch between the Circle of Fifths and Chromatic Circle. In 31 EDO, open the Circle List.

Shift + Click credit
Show Export panel

Shift-click the illustration credit to show or hide the Export panel.

Click illustration credit
Open About or Export

Hover over the lower credit and click About. Hold Shift while hovering to reveal Export, then click it.

Switch circle, or Toggle Circle List in microtonal world

In 12 EDO, switch between the fifths and chromatic circles. In 31 EDO, toggle the Circle List.

Shift +
Switch world

Switch between the 12-tone and 31-tone worlds.

Reverse circle direction

In 31-tone views, reverse the selected circle direction.
For example,
the reversed Circle of Fifths will be Circle of Fourths,
the reversed Circle of Major Thirds will be Circle of Minor Sixths, and
the reversed Chromatic Circle will be Circle of Diminished Octaves.

Toggle outer-ring labels

Applies globally to all 30 directed circles: numbers or note names.
Hover previews the alternate display, including while Shift previews the symmetry axis.

Shift +
Reset outer-ring labels

Restores each circle's default: numbers on most circles, relative-minor names on Fifths and Fourths.

Toggle theme

Switch between the light and dark themes.

Toggle hover sound

Turn note-hover sound on or off.
Sound is off by default.

Cycle soundwave

Cycle through Sine, Triangle, Square, and Sawtooth.

Toggle symmetry axis

Show or hide the symmetry axis and its reflected selection in the current world.
The last axis direction is remembered while hidden.

Swap primary and reflected selection

Make the reflected notes the primary selection across the current or last-used symmetry axis.
The previous primary selection becomes the new reflected selection.

[ / ]
Rotate selection and axis

Rotate the selected figure and symmetry axis together by one pitch position.
Shift moves both by two positions.
The physical keys work across keyboard layouts (for example, х / ъ on a Russian layout).

− / =
Rotate symmetry axis

Rotate the axis counterclockwise or clockwise by one direction.

Shift + − / =
Rotate axis by two directions

Rotate the symmetry axis by two directions.

Shift + Hover / Click
Preview or apply axis

Shift-hover previews the symmetry axis anywhere on the circle, including its centre.
Shift-click commits the preview.
Shift-click the committed axis again to remove it.

Toggle Harmony panel

Show or hide the 12 EDO Harmony reference panel in the 12-tone world.

Toggle Export panel

Show or hide the Export panel.
The current circle can be downloaded as an SVG or PDF file.

0–9, t, e
Select pitch classes

Select or deselect pitch classes 0–11 in 12 EDO.

Hover outer ring
Preview outer-ring labels

In 12 EDO, preview the corresponding pitch-class ID. In 31 EDO, temporarily switch the outer-ring labels between numbers and note names.

0–30, then Space / Enter
Enter a 31-EDO pitch ID

Enter a pitch ID from 0 to 30; it appears in the centre while typing. Press Space or Enter to select or deselect that pitch. While an ID is pending, note clicks still work and outer-ring hover can preview alternate labels.

Shift + ← / →
Move selection by two

Move the selected figure by two positions in the current circle's order, in either world.

↓ / ↑
Cycle 31-tone circles

In the 31-tone world, cycle forward or backward through 30 directed circle variants.
Hold the key to repeat at a maximum rate of once every 0.5 seconds.

Shift + ↓ / ↑
Cycle by two circles

Cycle forward or backward by two circle positions.
Holding the key repeats no faster than once every 0.5 seconds.

.
Switch 12-EDO spelling mode

Toggle between automatic chord-based spelling and manual enharmonic choices while keeping the same selected notes. In 31 EDO, this key continues to show or hide chord identification.

Backspace
Cancel or clear selection

Cancel a pending 31-tone number, or clear the current world selection.

While About Screen is open
Keyboard freezing

Functional shortcuts are disabled to prevent accidental actions. You can still toggle the theme.

,
Toggle About Screen

Show or hide this About screen.

Escape
Exit this About Screen

Close About and return to the current circle.
